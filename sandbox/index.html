The white dot on the cell shows where, its line which way the part points. 0° is the front (right), 180° the back. Tilt turns the part away from pointing straight out (a side flagellum pointing straight out pushes through the centre and can't turn the cell). Wire flagella and springs to a light sensor in the list below: an eyespot reads 1 facing straight up into the light, 0 facing sideways or down; lit above 0.5. Size: how much of the cell an oil droplet or vacuole fills, or how long a spine (up to 100 µm) or ejectisome (up to 200 µm) is. Oil's density is fixed (920, seawater 1025): a cell floats by holding more of it, about a quarter of its volume.
